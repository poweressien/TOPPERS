Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how TOPPERS collects, uses, and protects your personal information in compliance with the Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Full name, username, and email address (on registration)
  • Phone number (only when submitting an airtime redemption request)
  • Profile photo (optional, if uploaded)
  • Country of residence
  • Bio (optional)

Information collected automatically:

  • Game session data (questions answered, scores, time taken)
  • Login dates and streak activity
  • Device type and browser information (standard server logs)
  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention only)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your TOPPERS account
  • To track your points, achievements, and leaderboard position
  • To process airtime redemption requests via our payment partners
  • To send account notifications (login alerts, redemption confirmations)
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse of the rewards system
  • To improve the Platform's content and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations under Nigerian law

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for targeted advertising profiling.

3. Cookies & Tracking

TOPPERS uses essential session cookies to maintain your login state. We do not use tracking cookies, third-party advertising cookies, or behavioural profiling cookies.

If you have JavaScript enabled, we use localStorage to securely store your authentication token on your device. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to third-party servers.

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Payment processors (Paystack, VTPass): Your phone number and network are shared solely to process airtime redemptions.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose data when required by Nigerian law, court order, or regulatory authority.
  • Security: We may share data to investigate fraud, cheating, or Platform abuse.

5. Data Security

  • All passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms — we never store plain-text passwords.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption).
  • Authentication uses JWT tokens with short expiry periods.
  • Access to production databases is restricted to authorised personnel only.
  • Regular security audits are conducted to identify and address vulnerabilities.

6. Your Rights (NDPR)

Under the Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Portability — request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@toppers.ng. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your personal data will be removed within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes (e.g. airtime transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years per Nigerian financial regulations).

8. Children's Privacy

TOPPERS is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at support@toppers.ng and we will take prompt action to delete the information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify registered users via email. Your continued use of TOPPERS after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, please contact our Data Protection team:

📧 privacy@toppers.ng
Or visit our Contact Page.
