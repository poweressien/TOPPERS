About TOPPERS

A gamified learning and rewards platform built for Nigerians who believe knowledge should pay — literally.

Our Mission

TOPPERS was built with one goal: make learning rewarding in a real, tangible way. We believe that every Nigerian student, professional, and knowledge-lover deserves to be recognised and compensated for what they know.

By combining competitive quiz gameplay with real airtime rewards, we're turning idle screen time into productive, educational, and financially beneficial experiences.

What is TOPPERS?

TOPPERS is a free-to-play, web-based quiz and rewards platform. Players register, choose from 9 knowledge categories — including Mathematics, Science, Technology, Nigerian History, AI & Cybersecurity, and more — and compete in 5 unique game modes.

Every correct answer earns points. Points accumulate in your TOPPERS wallet. When you reach the redemption threshold, you can convert your points directly into mobile airtime on MTN, Airtel, Glo, or 9Mobile — sent instantly to your phone number.

Additional features include a daily login streak system, live 1v1 challenge battles, a global leaderboard, achievement badges, a referral programme, and more.
